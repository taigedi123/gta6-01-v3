Vermilion Bay is an original open-world crime game: Rico Vega comes home broke to a sun-soaked coastal city and runs twelve story jobs for the fixer Vera Solano — car theft, courier drops, gang clear-outs, a tail job, a repo run, an underground race, a casino vault heist and a final convoy hit on Don Sal Marconi — while stealing cars, outrunning the Bay PD through five wanted stars, roadblocks and a police helicopter, and building a life between jobs with taxi fares, courier runs, street races, vigilante hits, repo work, hidden cash stashes, a gun shop, a garage with upgrades and safehouses.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD, sprint with Shift, steal a car with E, aim with the mouse and shoot with a click, start a job with Q, pause with P.

## What's in the city

- **Open world:** 10 districts with distinct looks — Marina, Downtown, Calle Verde, North Hills, Docklands, Midtown, Foundry Row, Campus Green, Beachfront, Suburbs — plus a day/night cycle with golden-hour sunsets and night lighting, and weather that rolls in: rain that makes the roads slick, fog off the harbour, storms.
- **Vehicles:** sedan, taxi, van, sports car, motorbike, truck, police cruiser, limousine, muscle car and pickup, each with distinct handling; traffic, parked cars to steal, nitrous, skid marks, crashes and wrecks that burn.
- **Police:** 5-star wanted level, pursuing patrol cars that ram, roadblocks at 3 stars, a searchlight helicopter at 4 stars, and heat that cools when you break line of sight. Pedestrians flee gunfire and call the law.
- **Story:** 12 missions with briefings, timed drops, tail jobs, escapes and a finale, tracked on a HUD with a minimap, objective bar and mission markers.
- **Side work:** taxi fares, courier runs, street races, vigilante hits, repo jobs, street brawls, rival ambushes and random events (cash stashes, medkits, armour drops).
- **Progression:** cash, 7 weapons from the Iron & Oak gun shop (fists, pistol, shotgun, SMG, rifle, sniper, molotov) gated by street reputation, body armor, garage upgrades (turbo, plating, handling, nitrous, reinforced frame), a 5-car garage, safehouses and 3 save slots.
- **Feel:** WebAudio synth sound — gunshots, sirens, crashes, an engine hum and three radio stations — particles, screen shake, floating cash text and full title / pause / busted / mission-complete / shop / garage / stats / help screens.
